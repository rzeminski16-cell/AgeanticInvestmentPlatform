  • Tier 1 Recompute

    Re-runs the stored model with a changed input. The valuation, the scenarios and the sensitivity grid are already computed and recorded.

    Free · instant

    “What if the discount rate were half a point higher?”

  • Tier 2 Re-read

    A pass over everything already fetched and hashed for this company — filings, exhibits, pages — without fetching anything new.

    Pennies, shown with the answer

    “What does the 10-K say about lease obligations?”

  • Tier 3 Research

    Goes and gets new material. Everything fetched is hashed and joins this company's record, for this question and every later one.

    Priced, and approved by you first

    “Has anything changed at their main competitor?”
