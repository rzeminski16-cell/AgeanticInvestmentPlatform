Swapping the contents keeps the node — and the announcement — intact. **A zero is sent, and renders as nothing.** A pill reading "0" is noise on every page an operator has nothing waiting on, and `empty:hidden` in the nav's own class list is what makes an empty slot disappear rather than paint an empty circle. The swap still happens, which is the part that matters once a count is fetched more than once: it is what clears a number the operator has just acted on, where omitting the badge would leave the old one on screen. A badge whose provider failed is not here at all.
