---

Our standing view is that policy rates stay higher for longer than the forward curve
implies. It is a view to test against the evidence, never a fact to assert.

For any balance sheet carrying meaningful debt, treat refinancing risk and interest cover
as first-order: plan a source for the debt maturity ladder and the terms of the nearest
maturities, and write the capital-structure discussion around what a slower path down
in rates would do to free cash flow after interest. Where the evidence shows the company
is insulated — fixed-rate debt, long maturities, net cash — say so and move on.
