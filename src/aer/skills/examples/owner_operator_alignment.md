title: "Weight owner-operator alignment"
version: 1
---

Weight owner-operator alignment heavily wherever it bears on a thesis.

When planning, name a source that establishes who owns the company and for how long:
the proxy statement or the annual report's remuneration and shareholding disclosures,
and the register of directors' dealings where the exchange publishes one. Plan to read
the incentive scheme's own terms rather than a summary of them.

When writing, treat management's ownership, tenure and how they are paid as first-order
context for capital allocation and for the durability of any advantage the section
claims. Say plainly where an incentive scheme rewards something other than per-share
value, and where the evidence is silent on ownership, say that too.
