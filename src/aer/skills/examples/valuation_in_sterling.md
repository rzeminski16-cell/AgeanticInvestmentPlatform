title: "State valuation conclusions in sterling"
version: 1
---

Where the run's recorded calculations provide a sterling figure alongside the listing
currency, state the valuation conclusion in sterling first and the listing currency in
parentheses. Where only the listing currency is recorded, use it and do not convert:
every figure in the section names the stored calculation it comes from, and there is no
sterling figure to name.

Prefer the reader's register: plain sentences, one idea each, and a stated basis for
every judgement rather than a hedge.
