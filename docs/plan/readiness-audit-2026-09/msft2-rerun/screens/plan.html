What it intends to do

Revised plan for a 12-month standard note on Microsoft (MSFT, NASDAQ), as-of 2026-09-17, USD. Fiscal year ends 30 June, so the FY2026 10-K should be the anchor; the September 2026 quarter is unreported. Changes after review: (1) cash_flow_analysis now carries a recorded step-forward calculation converting disclosed construction-in-progress, leases signed but not yet commenced and purchase commitments into future depreciation and lease cost, flagged as arithmetic on disclosure rather than a forecast, and feeding scenarios_sensitivities; (2) earnings_quality pre-commits which relationships it computes and which direction of movement would support or undercut the flattering hypothesis, before evidence is seen; (3) every source line is marked CONFIRMED or PRESUMED with a named fallback; (4) the proxy source and the catalysts section are dropped, peer 10-Ks narrowed to stated useful lives, transcripts narrowed to quarters containing useful-life, capex or AI run-rate statements; (5) segment_analysis and capital_allocation now ask whether disclosed cloud/AI revenue is transacted with counterparties Microsoft has funded. This plan states no figures and asserts no facts; it names the source that would establish each.

Sections it will write

  • Earnings Quality Question (a), with the test pre-committed before evidence is gathered. Compute and record: (i) depreciation and amortisation over average gross PP&E plus finance-lease right-of-use assets, FY2024-FY2026; (ii) construction-in-progress as a share of gross PP&E; (iii) stated useful lives for server and network equipment across the three filings; (iv) segment operating margin trend against operating cash flow margin trend. The flattering hypothesis is supported if (i) falls while (ii) rises, or lives lengthen, or (iv) diverges with accrual margin rising and cash margin flat or falling; it is undercut if (i) is stable or rising while margin expands. Disclosure gaps go to validation_disagreements.
  • Cash Flow Analysis Two parts. Backward: operating cash flow, capex including finance-lease-funded additions, and free cash flow FY2024-FY2026, reconciling reported margin expansion to cash conversion. Forward: a recorded calculation stepping disclosed construction-in-progress, leases signed but not yet commenced, and multi-year capacity purchase commitments by maturity year into the depreciation and lease expense that lands inside the 12-month horizon, citing the filed note for each input and each timing or life assumption. Labelled arithmetic on disclosure, not a forecast; presented as a range where in-service timing is not disclosed.
  • Valuation — Discounted Cash Flow Question (b). Build the DCF from filed financials and market data as-of 2026-09-17, then invert it: state the revenue growth, operating margin and reinvestment path the prevailing enterprise value requires. Terminal assumptions, WACC inputs, share count and net debt must each trace to a stored fact or recorded calculation. The forward cost path from cash_flow_analysis enters as the near-term depreciation and lease expense input rather than a smoothed margin assumption.
  • Scenarios & Sensitivities Bound the inverted DCF over the inputs it is most sensitive to: cloud revenue deceleration, shortening of assumed equipment useful lives, and the step-forward depreciation and lease cost path running at the high end of its disclosed range with capex sustained but revenue not following. Present as a grid; state which cell corresponds to the as-of enterprise value. No single implied value is the output.
  • Segment Analysis Question (c). Segment revenue and operating income from the FY2026 10-K with FY2025 and FY2024 comparatives, noting any recast that breaks the series. Identify which AI-related revenue is separately disclosed versus only described qualitatively, and state explicitly where an AI revenue figure cannot be sourced from filed disclosure. Additionally ask whether the revenue and related-party notes disclose revenue from counterparties Microsoft has funded or committed capital to, and record that the disclosure is silent if it is.
  • Growth Outlook Durability of the growth mix over the horizon: remaining performance obligations and their disclosed recognition window, commercial bookings commentary, and capacity constraints described in risk factors and calls. Separate contracted backlog from expectation. Because no separate catalysts section is planned, list here the dated reporting events inside the horizon (Q1-Q3 FY2027 releases and 10-Qs, any disclosed capex guidance update) and the disclosure each would move.
  • Capital Allocation Capex versus buyback and dividend FY2024-FY2026 and the authorised repurchase status. Then the investment side of the funded-counterparty question: disclosed funding commitments, equity-method interests and partner arrangements, and whether their results sit inside or outside segment operating income. Assess whether reinvestment intensity is consistent with the returns the inverted DCF requires.
  • Key Risks Company and position risks, each sourced: depreciation- and lease-driven margin reversal as committed capacity comes into service, concentration of cloud or AI revenue in a small set of customers or funded partners, antitrust and other regulatory proceedings, and litigation or contingencies disclosed in the FY2026 commitments note.
  • Investment Thesis State the thesis as a small number of falsifiable claims, each tied to the evidence that would confirm or break it within 12 months, and each keyed to the pre-committed tests in earnings_quality and the grid cells in scenarios_sensitivities. No claim may rest on a figure not stored by another section.
  • Validation & Disagreements Record where sources conflict or a figure could not be sourced: management-described AI revenue versus filed segment disclosure, constant-currency growth versus reported dollars, vendor share counts versus filed counts, and every PRESUMED source line that did not materialise together with the weaker relationship used instead.
  • Executive Summary Condense the answers to the three operator questions and the resulting 12-month stance, including an explicit statement of which questions the filed evidence could not answer. Written last from facts and calculations already stored; no source of its own.

What it says it may get wrong

  • Microsoft is not known to report AI revenue as a separate segment line. Question (c) may be answerable only as a bounded range from RPO, Microsoft Cloud and management commentary, or not at all from filed evidence. This must be stated as a limitation, never resolved by estimation.
  • Three source lines are PRESUMED, not confirmed: PP&E class and construction-in-progress detail, related-party and partner funding disclosure, and leases-not-yet-commenced granularity. If any is absent, the named fallback is weaker evidence (ratio-level, aggregate, or silence recorded) and the quantitative core of question (a) becomes indirect.
  • The step-forward cost calculation requires in-service timing and asset-class lives that may not be disclosed. It is arithmetic on disclosure with stated assumptions and must be presented as a range, or dropped, if timing is not disclosable. It is not a forecast and must not be read as one.
  • Useful lives are a policy disclosure, not a reconciliation, and no asset-age detail is expected. A no-change disclosure leaves the depreciation-to-gross-PP&E relationship as the only test, which cannot separate fleet ageing from life assumptions.
  • If the FY2026 related-party and revenue notes are silent on revenue from funded counterparties, the independence of AI revenue cannot be established or refuted from filed evidence, and the section must record the silence.
  • Segment presentation has been recast in recent years. A further FY2026 recast would leave prior periods available only in restated form, breaking the as-originally-reported series.
  • The September 2026 quarter closes after the as-of date and is unreported, so the latest interim datapoint is the FY2026 10-K or its Q3. Intra-quarter deterioration is invisible to this run.
  • Inverting the DCF produces a required-assumption set, not a valuation conclusion, and is sensitive to terminal growth and WACC. The sensitivity grid, not any single implied value, is the output.
  • Deliberate narrowing, for the approver to accept or reject: the DEF 14A source and the catalysts section are dropped and the peer comparison cut to useful lives. The cost is no compensation-metric evidence on whether capitalisation choices are incentive-driven, and no standalone dated event calendar (reporting dates are folded into growth_outlook).
  • Constant-currency growth rates are not additive with reported dollar figures; any cross-use is invalid as a derived number and must be flagged in validation_disagreements.
