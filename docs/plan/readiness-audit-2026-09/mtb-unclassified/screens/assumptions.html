  • Exit multiple exit_multiple — 8.000000000000

    Not yet confirmed

    Proposed by aer.agents.assumptions, confidence 30%.

    EV/EBITDA is not how banks are priced — interest is operating revenue and debt is raw material, so enterprise value is near-meaningless here. 8x is calibrated by translation: regional banks trade near 10-12x earnings; at the derived 23.4% tax rate that is roughly 8x pre-tax, and D&A is small relative to earnings. Note the derived revenue series (5,992m to 1,657m) looks like a tagging change, so the EBITDA base this multiple is applied to may itself be distorted; a multiple cannot repair that. Of the two, I regard the perpetual-growth route as the better terminal estimate for this business, with 8x as a cross-check only.
