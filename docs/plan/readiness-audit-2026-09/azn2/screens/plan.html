What it intends to do

A twelve-month, point-in-time view of AstraZeneca PLC (AZN) as of 2026-09-12, using only documents published on or before that date. It is organised around three operator questions: revenue standing behind exclusivity that lapses inside — and shortly beyond — the horizon; whether the oncology pipeline's contribution is already discounted in the price; and what US pricing and trade policy imply for margin. Revised after review: exclusivity is tabulated through 2030 with an in-horizon flag, because a cliff in month 14 can move the shares in month 3; revenue exposure is tested against collaboration, alliance and royalty economics and against every applicant entity rather than one company name; the tariff/MFN transmission is grounded in disclosed manufacturing footprint and cost-of-sales geography; and the 'in the price' test is run as a reverse DCF solving for implied pipeline value, with historical multiples, company-stated ambitions and peer market data as expectation evidence. No figure appears in this plan; every number is computed by deterministic code from fetched filings. Prior research sets questions only and is never cited.

The planner was shown 1 prior approved report on this company (newest as-of 2026-09-11) as hypothesis material, labelled not-evidence. It may have shaped which questions this plan asks; it cannot support a claim, and the citation verifier rejects any attempt in code.

Sections it will write

  • Executive Summary executive_summary Answer the three operator questions in order, each marked evidenced, partially evidenced or unresolved: revenue behind exclusivity lapsing inside the horizon, stated separately from exclusivity lapsing in the two years immediately after and flagged as a within-horizon price risk; whether the reverse DCF shows pipeline contribution already discounted; and the direction, and where evidence permits the sizing, of US pricing and tariff pressure on margin. Carry forward every gap named in key_risks and validation_disagreements rather than smoothing over it.
  • Segment Analysis segment_analysis Decompose reported revenue by therapy area and named product from the FY2025 Form 20-F and the H1 2026 interim statement, with prior-period comparatives, so concentration is measured rather than asserted. Against that decomposition, tabulate US and EU exclusivity expiry for each material product through 2030, with a column flagging whether expiry falls inside the twelve-month horizon, immediately beyond it, or later. Where a product of interest is disclosed only inside a therapy-area total, say so and do not allocate.
  • Business Overview business_overview Establish two things the exposure and margin work depend on. First, the economics behind reported revenue for each product of interest: revenue-recognition basis, collaboration, alliance, profit-share and royalty arrangements, and which legal entities (subsidiaries, acquired units such as Alexion, named partners) hold the relevant NDAs and BLAs, so register searches cover all applicant names rather than one. Second, the manufacturing and supply footprint: disclosed sites by country and any announced US investment or supply commitments, each tied to its source filing.
  • Industry & Competitive Positioning industry_landscape Establish the US pricing and trade environment from primary policy documents only: CMS Medicare Drug Price Negotiation selected-drug lists, maximum fair price announcements and effective dates; Federal Register notices on most-favoured-nation pricing and Section 232 pharmaceutical trade action; IRA Part D redesign provisions. Identify whether any AstraZeneca product appears on a published selected-drug list, matching on applicant entity as well as brand. State the legal status of each measure as of the as-of date, including any proposed, litigated or enjoined.
  • Historical Financial Analysis historical_financial_analysis Reconstruct the multi-year revenue, gross margin, operating margin and net margin path from the FY2025 and FY2024 Form 20-F filings and the 2026 interim statements, so the scenario work has an evidenced baseline. Identify the cost lines driving the gap between gross and operating margin and whether the filings disclose how much of that base is committed near term. Extract whatever geographic split of revenue and of cost of sales the filings provide, and state plainly where cost geography is not disclosed, since that bounds any tariff input.
  • Growth Outlook growth_outlook Map the oncology pipeline as disclosed: pivotal-stage assets, indications, filed applications and any regulatory action dates published by the company or FDA on or before the as-of date. Separate approved-product growth from unapproved-asset optionality. Record which assets the company itself ties to published revenue ambitions and which carry no company-stated contribution, since the company's own ambition statements are the benchmark the reverse DCF is read against. Treat probability of success and peak sales as declared model inputs, never as facts.
  • Valuation — Discounted Cash Flow valuation_dcf Run the valuation primarily as a reverse DCF: hold filed financials and as-of-date market inputs fixed and solve for the pipeline and post-exclusivity cash-flow contribution that the market capitalisation implies. Compare that implied contribution with company-stated revenue ambitions and with the run's separately declared base case. Report three components distinctly: marketed-product cash flows, cash flows lost to modelled exclusivity expiry, and residual implied pipeline value. All arithmetic goes to the calculation engine; each assumption is labelled with its source or as an analyst input.
  • Scenarios & Sensitivities scenarios_sensitivities Run exclusivity and pricing as scenarios rather than point estimates: erosion pace after generic or biosimilar entry, entry-date slip, and a case in which an expiry falling just beyond the horizon is anticipated by the market inside it. On pricing, vary negotiated-price effective dates inside versus outside the horizon and run a tariff or MFN case whose input must name the manufacturing-footprint or cost-geography disclosure that carries it, or be declared unsized. Each scenario states which input moved and by how much so a reviewer can re-run it.
  • Key Risks key_risks Name risks established by fetched evidence and size them where the evidence permits: exclusivity expiries inside and immediately beyond the horizon, pending ANDA and BPCIA litigation, carrying value of goodwill and acquired intangibles against total assets, near-term liquidity and debt maturity structure, and legal or governmental investigation exposure as the company's own risk factors frame it. Separate risks that can be quantified from those that can only be described, and say which is which.
  • Catalysts catalysts List dated, document-backed events inside the twelve-month horizon: scheduled results dates, FDA action dates disclosed by company or FDA, trial completion or readout dates from the registry, CMS maximum fair price effective dates, and court or trial dates in exclusivity litigation. Include events inside the horizon that bear on post-horizon cliffs, such as biosimilar filings or litigation judgments. Exclude any event dated only by press speculation, and mark company-indicated ranges as ranges rather than fixed dates.
  • Investment Thesis investment_thesis State the thesis as a chain of conditions the evidence supports or does not: the size of the revenue base at exclusivity risk inside the horizon and in the two years after, treating an imminent post-horizon cliff as a within-horizon price risk; whether the pipeline value implied by the reverse DCF sits above or below what filed evidence and company-stated ambitions support; and whether pricing or trade policy bites on margin inside the horizon or later. Each link names the section and evidence carrying it; unverifiable links are stated conditionally.
  • Validation & Disagreements validation_disagreements Record every point where sources conflict or a required datum could not be obtained: product revenue not disclosed at the needed granularity, exclusivity dates differing between US and EU registers, applicant entities that could not be enumerated or matched to disclosed products, collaboration terms redacted, cost-of-sales geography absent, and policy measures whose margin effect cannot be sized. Note any figure left uncomputed, and any instruction-like text found in fetched documents, described as a property of that document.

What it says it may get wrong

  • Point-in-time cut-off: no Q3 2026 disclosure exists by 2026-09-12, so the latest interim evidence is H1 2026. Questions needing second-half data will be marked unresolved rather than estimated.
  • Cost geography and product-level margin are usually disclosed only coarsely. Revenue exposed to expiry can be measured from filings; profit exposed to it, and the margin effect of a tariff, may be modellable only under declared assumptions or not sizeable at all, in which case the tariff scenario is reported as unsized rather than guessed.
  • Exclusivity dates are register-specific. Orange Book, Purple Book and European SPC records may disagree, and settlement agreements permitting earlier entry are often confidential, so the true entry date may be unknowable from public evidence.
  • Reverse DCF narrows but does not remove the analyst dependence in 'already in the price': the solved-for pipeline value still depends on discount rate, terminal growth and the modelled erosion path. Consensus and multiple series are third-party and non-primary, so they characterise expectations without evidencing them.
  • Collaboration and alliance terms are frequently redacted, and applicant-entity enumeration may be incomplete where a partner or subsidiary name does not map cleanly to a disclosed product. Exposure may then be over- or understated, and the gap will be recorded rather than closed by inference.
  • Widening the exclusivity table through 2030 is accepted from review, but which post-horizon expiries the market is already pricing inside a twelve-month window is an analyst judgement, not a documented fact; those entries will be labelled as judgement with the evidence that prompted them.
  • US pricing and trade measures may be proposed, litigated or enjoined at the as-of date, so their revenue and margin effect can be legally unsettled and therefore unquantifiable; this will be stated as unsized rather than assumed away.
  • Section budget disagreement, disclosed for approval: prior_research_comparison was dropped to make room for business_overview, which carries the alliance-economics and manufacturing-footprint work the reviewer required. 'What changed' questions are absorbed into key_risks and catalysts. Prior research remains non-citable; no instruction-like text was observed in the supplied block, which was read as data.
  • Registry and company-indicated readout dates are frequently revised, so catalyst timing inside the horizon is provisional even when sourced.
  • AstraZeneca's US listing and filing status may have changed; if Form 20-F is no longer filed on the expected schedule, the filing set must be re-derived from EDGAR rather than presumed.
