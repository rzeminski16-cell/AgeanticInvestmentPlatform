What it intends to do

Plan for a standard 12-month note on AstraZeneca PLC (AZN ADR, NYSE), USD, as of 2026-09-17. AstraZeneca is a UK foreign private issuer: the evidence base is the Form 20-F (IFRS) plus unaudited half-year and quarterly announcements and LSE RNS, not 10-K/10-Q; verified on EDGAR before anything else. Revised after review: (1) LOE exposure is built product by product from the 20-F revenue analysis, with the geographic disclosure read first to size ex-US revenue, US dates from Orange Book, Purple Book and the FDA Paragraph IV list, ex-US dates from EMA product information and SPC/EPO registers where obtainable, each tag recording the application number and holder it was matched on, and collaboration or profit-share revenue separated from own-product sales before concentration is computed; (2) 'already in the price' is answered by a reverse DCF solving for the pipeline contribution implied by the as-of ADR price, with consensus forecasts as a separate cross-check; (3) the pricing question covers IRA mechanics plus Medicaid rebate and 340B, MFN or reference-pricing actions and medicine tariffs, distinguishing rules in force from proposals. No figures are asserted here; code computes every number from retrieved facts.

Sections it will write

  • Segment Analysis Read the geographic revenue disclosure first to establish the US versus ex-US split, then decompose revenue by therapy area and product from the FY2025 20-F and H1 2026 announcement. Separate collaboration, alliance and profit-share revenue from own-product sales before any concentration figure is computed. Tag each material product with exclusivity status, recording for each tag the application or licence number and the named holder it was matched on, and flagging divested or out-licensed products. Report ex-US status only where an independent register supports it; otherwise mark as company narrative. State whether IFRS 8 reportable segments are more aggregated than the therapy areas used.
  • Industry & Competitive Positioning Map US pricing channels to this issuer, stating for each whether it is in force, proposed, or inapplicable as of the as-of date: Medicare negotiation selected-drug lists and maximum fair price effective dates; Part D redesign and manufacturer discount obligations; inflation rebates; Medicaid rebate and 340B changes; most-favoured-nation or reference-pricing executive actions; and Section 232 or other tariffs on imported medicines, tied to the manufacturing footprint established in business_overview. Cover biosimilar and generic entry dynamics in the relevant classes. Distinguish published policy parameters from any inference about net realised price, which is not disclosed.
  • Growth Outlook Pipeline and launch-driven growth over the horizon. From the company pipeline table and clinical trials appendix, list phase III assets and regulatory submissions with expected readout or decision inside the horizon, prioritising oncology, marking each timing as company-disclosed or registry-derived. Identify disclosed FY2026 guidance and its stated basis. Flag probability of technical and regulatory success as an assumption input, not filed evidence.
  • Valuation — Discounted Cash Flow Build the DCF from filed IFRS financials: revenue, core and reported operating margin, R&D, tax, capex and working capital from the FY2025 20-F and FY2024 comparative; capital structure and maturities from the borrowings note; share count and ADR ratio from the 20-F. Split value into on-market products versus pipeline. Then run the step the operator's second question requires: a reverse DCF solving, from the as-of ADR price, for the pipeline contribution the market implies. Present consensus forecasts only as a separate, secondary cross-check of analyst rather than market expectations. Discount rate and terminal growth declared as assumptions.
  • Scenarios & Sensitivities Scenarios built on the operator's questions: erosion curves per product losing or contesting exclusivity inside the horizon, run separately for US and ex-US where dates differ; pricing scenarios by channel, keeping in-force rules separate from proposals; pipeline scenarios conditioned on named phase III outcomes. Sensitivity of enterprise value and of the implied pipeline contribution to discount rate, terminal growth and gross margin. Product-level margin flow-through is modelled, not sourced, and must be labelled as such on the output.
  • Key Risks Risks drawn from the FY2025 20-F risk factors and legal proceedings note: paragraph IV and biosimilar litigation, patent invalidation and SPC challenges, pipeline failure, manufacturing and supply concentration and tariff exposure, China and emerging-market pricing including volume-based procurement, FX translation, government investigations, and acquired-intangible impairment from prior acquisitions. Each risk attributed to the disclosure that raises it.
  • Catalysts Dated events inside the 12 months to Sept 2027: scheduled results announcements, company-disclosed regulatory decision dates, named phase III readouts, CMS maximum fair price effective dates, rulemaking comment and effective dates, and known generic or biosimilar entry dates. Each with the source establishing the date, and a note where the date is indicative rather than fixed.
  • Historical Financial Analysis Multi-year revenue, gross margin, core and reported operating margin, R&D and SG&A intensity and EPS trajectory from the FY2025 and FY2024 20-Fs, with H1 2026 as latest interim. Purpose is a measured baseline for the margin-outlook question, including the historical gap between reported and core measures and the historical share of revenue from collaboration arrangements.
  • Earnings Quality Examine the reported-versus-core reconciliation, acquired intangible amortisation and impairment, collaboration and milestone revenue recognition, capitalisation policy for development and intangibles, and cash conversion against reported profit. Establish whether the margin baseline feeding the valuation is durable or flattered by adjusting items.
  • Investment Thesis State the thesis and its falsifiers against the three operator questions: LOE-exposed revenue quantified in segment_analysis with its mapping caveats, pipeline contribution implied by price versus modelled value from valuation_dcf, and channel-by-channel pricing path from industry_landscape. Every claim references a stored fact or recorded calculation; each leg carries the specific observation that would break it.
  • Business Overview Corporate structure and subsidiary holders relevant to product attribution, listing arrangement and ADR ratio, reporting framework and presentation currency as stated in the filing, principal therapy areas, and the manufacturing and supply footprint by country and site — the last being the input tariff exposure is assessed against. Include the acquisition history shaping the current intangible base.
  • Executive Summary Compressed answer to the three operator questions with explicit confidence levels and pointers to the sections and facts behind each, including whether ex-US exclusivity was independently sourced or narrowed to US only. Written last, adds no new claims, marks residual judgement as judgement.

What it says it may get wrong

  • Product-to-application mapping can break question one in either direction: Orange Book and Purple Book are keyed to application numbers and named holders (often subsidiaries or acquired entities such as Alexion), while the 20-F revenue table is keyed to product names. Each LOE tag must record the application number and holder matched on, and unmatched or divested/out-licensed products must be shown as unmatched rather than silently included or excluded.
  • Revenue booked as collaboration, alliance or profit-share is not own-product sales and does not erode on the same terms. It must be separated from product revenue before any LOE concentration is computed, and the split may be disclosed only in aggregate, leaving part of the exposure unattributable.
  • Commercial loss of exclusivity is not a disclosed date. Registers give patent, SPC and regulatory-exclusivity dates by jurisdiction, but actual erosion depends on pending litigation, confidential settlements and at-risk launches. LOE-exposed revenue is a range, not a figure.
  • EU/UK exclusivity may not be independently establishable for every product: SPC records are national, unevenly published and keyed to patents rather than products. Where EMA and SPC/EPO evidence is unobtainable, segment_analysis narrows to US exclusivity and states the ex-US revenue share left uncovered rather than leaning on company narrative.
  • AstraZeneca does not disclose gross or operating margin by product. The margin impact of a specific product's exclusivity loss or negotiated price cannot be computed from filed data and must be modelled, with the assumption stated on the face of the output.
  • Net realised US prices are not public: gross-to-net rebates, 340B and payer discounts are undisclosed. Published maximum fair prices and list prices bound the pricing question rather than answer it.
  • Several pricing channels may be proposals rather than law at the as-of date, and tariff scope for medicines can change after it. Tariff exposure also depends on which sites supply US volumes, which filings may disclose only as a site list — so exposure is directional, not quantified, and each channel must be labelled in force, proposed or inapplicable.
  • The reverse DCF gives the pipeline contribution implied by the ADR price only conditional on the modelled on-market base, discount rate and terminal growth; a different base shifts the implied residual. If the consensus licence yields no product-level oncology forecasts, the second operator question is answered from the reverse DCF alone as a sensitivity band against disclosed pipeline scale, with the weaker evidence base stated.
  • AstraZeneca is a foreign private issuer reporting under IFRS: there is no 10-K or 10-Q, so interim figures come from unaudited announcements with weaker assurance. The FY2025 20-F filing date must be verified on EDGAR; if unavailable, the audited baseline becomes FY2024 and the report says so rather than substituting interim data.
  • Q3 2026 results fall after 2026-09-17, leaving roughly a quarter unobserved. Separately, IFRS 8 segments may be more aggregated than the therapy areas needed, and China and emerging-market pricing exposure including volume-based procurement is disclosed only at regional level, so product-level exposure to those regimes cannot be established from filings.
