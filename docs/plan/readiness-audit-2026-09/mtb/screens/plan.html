What it intends to do

Plan for a 12-month-horizon standard research run on M&T Bank Corporation (MTB, NYSE), point-in-time as of 2026-09-12, base currency USD. It is organised around three operator questions: CRE concentration and how it is reserved; whether net interest margin is at a peak and what deposit repricing and the hedge book do to it; whether ROTCE supports the price-to-tangible-book multiple. Evidence comes first from MTB filings dated on or before the as-of date (FY2025 10-K, Q1 and Q2 2026 10-Qs, earnings 8-Ks), then from an explicit sweep of every 8-K and IR posting between 2026-07-01 and 2026-09-12 with 'no qualifying disclosure found' recorded as an affirmative result, then from regulatory sources (FR Y-9C/Call Report line items, 2026 DFAST and stress capital buffer) and peer filings. Concentration ratios and all metrics are recorded as calculations over stored line items under declared definitions, never as retrieved figures. No figure is stated in this plan; each section names the schedule that would establish it. Before retrieval, each quantitative section's calculation contract is checked for bank-specific metrics; a mismatch is escalated, not substituted with a non-bank proxy.

Sections it will write

  • Executive Summary executive_summary Condense answers to the three operator questions into verified statements with explicit pointers: CRE share of loans and its reserve coverage; direction and drivers of NIM including deposit cost, asset repricing and hedge contribution; ROTCE versus P/TBV. State the data cut (latest filed period before 2026-09-12) and the result of the 2026-07-01 to 2026-09-12 disclosure sweep, including a negative finding. Flag which conclusions rest on management forward-looking commentary rather than filed data. No figure may originate here; every number is a reference to a stored fact or recorded calculation.
  • Balance Sheet & Liquidity balance_sheet_liquidity Core for question (a). From the FY2025 10-K loan note and the Q2 2026 10-Q: loan composition by category — commercial real estate, construction/land, multifamily, owner- versus non-owner-occupied — plus the ACL rollforward, allocation by portfolio segment, charge-offs, nonaccrual and criticized/classified balances. Then the inputs to question (b): deposit composition (noninterest- versus interest-bearing), time deposit maturity ladder, uninsured share, brokered funding, borrowings, and the disclosed repricing/maturity distribution of loans and securities. Flag any dimension the filings do not disclose.
  • Earnings Quality earnings_quality Decompose net interest income and margin from the average balance sheet and rate/volume schedules in the 10-K and 10-Qs: earning-asset yields, interest-bearing deposit cost, purchase-accounting accretion from the People's United transaction, securities repositioning, and items management labels notable. Add the derivative and hedge disclosures: received-fixed and pay-fixed notionals by maturity, designated cash-flow and fair-value hedge effects on interest income and expense, and amounts reclassified from AOCI. Identify how much of margin and ROTCE is recurring. Use the company's own non-GAAP ROTCE and tangible book reconciliation rather than restating it.
  • Segment Analysis segment_analysis Use MTB's reported operating segments (including Commercial Real Estate, Commercial Banking, Retail Banking, Institutional Services and Wealth Management) from the 10-K segment note and quarterly updates to establish where CRE risk and where fee income actually sit: segment average loans, deposits, net interest income and provision, plus the geographic footprint concentration disclosed in the filings. Note any segment re-presentation that breaks comparability with prior years.
  • Historical Financial Analysis historical_financial_analysis Build the multi-year series needed to judge whether NIM and ROTCE sit at cyclical extremes: NIM, efficiency ratio, ROTCE, ACL-to-loans, net charge-off rate and CRE balances across the FY2022-FY2025 10-Ks plus 2026 interim periods. Record definitional breaks (acquisition accounting, CECL model changes, segment re-presentation) so trends are on consistent bases. Confirm before retrieval that this section's calculation contract accepts bank metrics rather than requiring leverage, working capital or EV-based ratios; escalate if it does not.
  • Capital Allocation capital_allocation CET1 and other regulatory ratios, the 2026 stress capital buffer, repurchase authorisation and actual repurchases, dividend declarations, and AOCI drag on tangible common equity. Establish whether capital return is constrained by CRE stress loss assumptions in the Federal Reserve's 2026 supervisory results. Include any capital action disclosed by 8-K between 2026-07-01 and the as-of date. Sources: 10-K/10-Q capital notes, 8-K capital plan disclosures, Fed DFAST publication.
  • Industry & Competitive Positioning industry_landscape Position MTB against regional-bank peers on the operator's metrics: CRE-to-total-risk-based-capital concentration measured against the interagency 100%/300% guidance under a declared construction of the numerator; cumulative interest-bearing deposit beta; ROTCE and P/TBV. Use peer Q2 2026 10-Qs for the same period end and each peer's own non-GAAP reconciliation, noting where definitions differ so comparisons are not made across incompatible bases. Escalate if the section contract only supports non-bank comparables.
  • Valuation — Discounted Cash Flow valuation_dcf Question (c). A bank has no meaningful enterprise free cash flow, so valuation must run equity-side: residual income / excess return on tangible common equity, dividend discount, and a P/TBV-versus-ROTCE cross-check against peers. Inputs are stored facts — tangible common equity, share count, market price as of 2026-09-12 — with cost of equity and sustainable ROTCE as declared assumptions, not asserted facts. State explicitly if the section's calculation contract only supports an FCFF structure, and escalate rather than force a non-bank proxy.
  • Scenarios & Sensitivities scenarios_sensitivities Grids on the swing variables: (i) rate path — cumulative interest-bearing deposit beta and noninterest-bearing mix shift, combined with fixed-rate asset repricing and the maturity roll-off of the swap book, using the company's own disclosed interest-rate sensitivity simulation; (ii) CRE credit — loss severity and criticized-asset migration applied to disclosed CRE balances, benchmarked to supervisory stress loss rates. Effects on NIM, provision, ROTCE and tangible book per share reported via recorded calculations only, with hedge-notional data flagged where undisclosed.
  • Investment Thesis investment_thesis State bull and bear cases as testable propositions tied to evidence. Bear: CRE credit migration and reserve build, plus deposit repricing and expiring received-fixed hedges compressing spread. Bull: CRE runoff already reducing concentration, fee income mix, capital return at a discount to peer P/TBV. Each leg names the disclosure that would confirm or falsify it and the horizon over which it resolves — the next two quarterly filings inside the 12-month window.
  • Key Risks key_risks Company-level risks evidenced from the 10-K risk factors and disclosed exposures: office and multifamily concentration, geographic concentration in the Northeast/Mid-Atlantic footprint, uninsured deposit share and funding sensitivity, AOCI and held-to-maturity securities marks, hedge roll-off and basis risk, regulatory and stress-capital risk, and integration or acquisition risk. Distinguish risks the filings quantify from those they only describe.
  • Catalysts catalysts Dated events inside the 12-month horizon that would resolve the thesis: Q3 2026 and Q4 2026 earnings releases and the 10-Q/10-K with updated CRE criticized balances and ACL, the 2027 stress test cycle and buffer reset, capital plan and buyback announcements, scheduled investor conference appearances, and FOMC decisions driving the deposit repricing path. Give expected timing windows and mark each as scheduled or conditional.

What it says it may get wrong

  • Contract mismatch is not confined to valuation_dcf. historical_financial_analysis, industry_landscape, scenarios_sensitivities and earnings_quality may have output contracts built for non-financial issuers (EV multiples, leverage, working capital, FCF) and may lack NIM, ROTCE, ACL-to-loans and deposit beta. Each quantitative section's contract must be checked before retrieval; where bank metrics are not accepted, escalate rather than validate on an irrelevant proxy.
  • The quarter ending 2026-09-30 is unreported at the as-of date, so the freshest periodic data is the Q2 2026 10-Q. The 8-K and IR sweep for 2026-07-01 to 2026-09-12 bounds but does not remove this gap: credit migration and deposit repricing that triggered no disclosure obligation remain invisible and must be labelled as unobserved, not inferred.
  • CRE granularity may not exist at the level the question implies. Office versus multifamily versus industrial splits, LTV, debt-service coverage and maturity ladders are often disclosed only in investor decks or annually, and the ACL is generally not allocated by property type.
  • Hedge-book disclosure may be insufficient for question (b). Filings may give aggregate notionals without a maturity ladder, or not separate hedge-related amounts within interest income and expense. Where that is so, the hedge contribution to the forward NIM path cannot be quantified and must be stated as undisclosed rather than modelled.
  • 'Peak' NIM is inherently forward-looking. Filings support only realised margin; any peak call rests on management guidance and on scenario assumptions about deposit beta, asset repricing and hedge roll-off, which are declared inputs and not evidence.
  • FR Y-9C and Call Report data for 2026Q2 may not be posted or may be amended later. Separately, the interagency concentration ratio computed here from declared schedule lines is our construction under published guidance and may differ from any measure supervisors use internally; it must be presented as a calculation, with its definition shown.
  • Peer ROTCE and tangible book definitions differ on intangibles, preferred equity and AOCI treatment, so the P/TBV-versus-ROTCE comparison risks being not like-for-like unless each peer's own reconciliation is used and differences are recorded.
  • Point-in-time enforcement is fragile on market and peer data feeds, which commonly return restated or current-vintage values. Any source that cannot deliver an as-of-2026-09-12 snapshot must be flagged rather than silently used.
  • Purchase-accounting accretion from prior acquisitions distorts both NIM and ROTCE trend comparisons; if recent filings stop disclosing accretion separately, the recurring-earnings split in earnings_quality cannot be completed for those periods.
  • If the 8-K/IR sweep finds an acquisition, securities repositioning or portfolio sale announced close to the as-of date, trailing balances become unrepresentative of the forward book. This run cannot construct pro-forma statements; the finding would be reported as a limitation on the 12-month view.
