What it intends to do

Quick-mode, 12-month review of MSFT as of 2026-09-17 answering three operator questions: (1) is cloud margin expansion economic or an artefact of capitalising infrastructure, (2) what must be true for the current multiple, (3) how much growth is AI revenue absent in FY2023. This plan states no figures; all numbers come from filed evidence and recorded calculations. Revised after review to add: ASC 606 revenue disaggregation, unearned revenue and remaining performance obligations (with disclosed recognition split) as the durability test; explicit enterprise-value bridge inputs (cash and short-term investments, total debt, operating and finance lease liabilities, equity-method carrying values, diluted share count, buybacks, stock-based compensation); a pre-committed reverse-DCF solve protocol so 'what would have to be true' is falsifiable; a fallback rule when no cloud-level operating margin is disclosed; and core/optional source tiering. Optional sources (peer 10-Ks, DEF 14A, full transcript series) are dropped before any core retrieval is abbreviated. Sections below are ordered by priority under a time box.

Sections it will write

  • Executive Summary Direct answers to the three operator questions, each tagged with its evidence class (audited filing, unaudited company disclosure, management assertion, unverified proposal). Where filings cannot settle a question, say so plainly: if no cloud-level operating margin is derivable from segment disclosure, state that it was not derivable rather than constructing one; if no audited AI revenue line exists, say the question is bounded, not resolved. Name the variables held fixed in the reverse DCF and where their values came from. Carry forward the single largest disagreement surfaced by validation.
  • Historical Financial Analysis FY2023-FY2026 consolidated and segment revenue, operating income and margin from the 10-Ks, as-reported and recast where disclosed. Retrieve the ASC 606 revenue disaggregation by product/service line as the closest audited proxy for which lines could contain AI offerings. Durability test: track remaining performance obligations and unearned revenue growth, with disclosed recognition-period split, against reported revenue growth across the same years. Retrieve D&A, cost of revenue composition, stock-based compensation as a recorded line, and any quantified effect of useful-life changes. Fallback rule: if no cloud-level operating margin is disclosed, report reportable-segment margin as presented plus any company-defined Microsoft Cloud gross margin percentage, labelled unaudited. Flag every derived ratio as a recorded calculation.
  • Cash Flow Analysis Core of the capitalisation question. Operating cash flow, additions to property and equipment, finance-lease additions, non-cash depreciation, stock-based compensation and share repurchases from FY2024-FY2026 cash flow statements and FY2026 10-Qs. Compare capex growth with depreciation growth and with reported cloud-related revenue growth to test whether margin leads or lags the cash cost of capacity. Show the quarterly trajectory annual figures smooth over. Record explicitly where filings do not split datacentre shell, land, leased capacity and server spend.
  • Valuation — Discounted Cash Flow Reverse DCF of implied expectations, not a fair value. Pre-committed solve protocol: hold discount rate (date-stamped Treasury yield plus a documented ERP source) and terminal growth fixed at sourced values; fix capex intensity and operating margin at the most recent disclosed period levels; solve for one variable at a time and report a stated grid with every held value and its source named. Enterprise-value bridge from the FY2026 10-K balance sheet, debt and lease notes: cash and short-term investments, total debt, operating and finance lease liabilities, equity-method carrying values. Diluted shares govern; use the most recent date-stamped count available and disclose its date versus 2026-09-17. Note terminal value dominance versus the 12-month horizon.
  • Balance Sheet & Liquidity Two jobs. First, the capitalised-infrastructure question: gross and net property and equipment, accumulated depreciation, construction in progress, disclosed useful lives, and operating versus finance lease liabilities and maturities from the FY2026 10-K notes, establishing how much infrastructure cost sits on the balance sheet awaiting depreciation. Second, supply the enterprise-value bridge items to valuation_dcf: cash and short-term investments, total and current debt with maturities, lease liabilities, and equity-method or unconsolidated investments with related commitments that sit outside consolidated margin.
  • Business Overview Define reportable segments exactly as presented in the FY2026 10-K segment note and identify every recast or reallocation versus FY2023-FY2025 presentation, since comparability is the crux of the AI-growth question. Map the ASC 606 disaggregation categories to segments and identify which disclosed revenue lines could contain AI offerings. Mark which metrics (Microsoft Cloud revenue, Azure growth rates, any AI run-rate) are company-defined, non-GAAP or unaudited, and note that no reportable segment maps one-to-one to 'Microsoft Cloud'.
  • Investment Thesis State the thesis as falsifiable conditions over the 12-month horizon, each linked to the filing line or note that would confirm or break it at the next two reporting dates: direction of reportable-segment operating margin (or the fallback metric if no cloud margin is disclosed), capex-to-revenue trajectory, depreciation growth relative to segment revenue growth, remaining performance obligation growth relative to reported revenue growth, and whether AI-related revenue disclosure improves. No price target.
  • Key Risks Risks to the investment case, ordered by materiality to the three operator questions: depreciation catch-up as capacity vintages mature, disclosed capex and purchase commitments, counterparty or customer concentration in AI arrangements, economics held in equity-method or unconsolidated vehicles, and disclosure risk if segment or metric definitions change again. Cite the filing source for each; label any risk not evidenced in filings as a proposal for verification.
  • Validation & Disagreements Record conflicts between sources: audited 10-K figures versus press-release or slide-deck metrics, management characterisations of AI revenue scale versus what segment and ASC 606 disaggregation support, recast-versus-as-reported mismatches across FY2023-FY2026, and any inconsistency between RPO recognition-period disclosure and management commentary on backlog duration. State which source governs and why, and list the questions the evidence leaves open.

What it says it may get wrong

  • No audited 'AI revenue' line exists in MSFT filings and the term has no standard definition. Operator question three can be bounded only by ASC 606 disaggregation and attributed management commentary, not resolved with a verified figure.
  • A reverse DCF is underdetermined: one observed price does not fix a unique combination of growth, margin, capex intensity, discount rate and terminal growth. The pre-committed protocol (fixed sourced discount rate and terminal growth, margin and capex intensity at disclosed recent levels, one variable solved at a time, held values named) makes the output falsifiable but still yields a grid of conditions, not one answer. Terminal value also dominates and extends far beyond the 12-month horizon.
  • No reportable segment maps to 'Microsoft Cloud' and depreciation may not be allocated to a cloud-level unit. Pre-committed fallback: report reportable-segment margin as presented plus any company-defined Microsoft Cloud gross margin percentage, labelled unaudited, and state in the executive summary that a cloud operating margin was not derivable. No constructed cloud margin will be published.
  • Segment presentation has been recast in recent fiscal years. If FY2023 figures are not restated onto the current basis in any available filing, three-year growth comparisons are partly non-comparable and must be reported as such.
  • Filings may not disaggregate capex between datacentre land and shell, leased capacity and server hardware. The attribution needed to fully separate mix from unit economics in the margin question may not be derivable from disclosed data.
  • RPO is an imperfect durability test: it may include non-cloud commitments, exclude cancellable and short-term contracts, carry no AI split, and disclose only a coarse recognition-period bucket. It bounds contracted revenue but cannot isolate AI or guarantee margin.
  • Effects of changes in estimated useful lives are typically quantified only in the year of change and not retrospectively restated, limiting how far the depreciation-policy effect on margin can be traced across FY2023-FY2026.
  • Share count staleness: no share count is date-stamped to 2026-09-17. Cover-page counts are dated at filing and diluted weighted-average counts are period figures, so the EV bridge will carry a dated count with its date and basis disclosed rather than an adjusted estimate.
  • If the FY2026 10-K is not yet filed or indexed on EDGAR as of 2026-09-17, the work falls back to the FY2025 10-K plus FY2026 10-Qs, leaving the most recent period unaudited and the fiscal-year picture incomplete.
  • Quick-mode time box: optional sources (peer 10-Ks, DEF 14A, the FY2024-FY2025 transcript series) are dropped before core retrieval is thinned. The approver should note this removes the peer depreciation-policy cross-check, weakening one line of evidence on the flattered-margin question.
