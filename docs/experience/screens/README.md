# The drawn screens

Nine artboards, one per surface, drawn in the platform's own **Tracework** design system —
teal where something can be inspected, amber where a person must choose, every colour paired
with a word. Every token is lifted from
[`../../redesign/01-design-system.md`](../../redesign/01-design-system.md); nothing here
invents a colour, a size or a radius.

The canvas: **https://claude.ai/code/artifact/4af01758-27d6-4c34-9f5c-d9283d6f44c7**

| Research and evidence | Research run · Report reader · Ask | §7, §8, §13 |

`_shared.css` carries the tokens every artboard imports. These are **static mockups** — nothing
is wired, and no figure in them comes from a real run. They exist to settle layout, hierarchy
and wording, and the specifications are normative where the two disagree.
