Valuation

The discounted cash flow values the equity at $485.29 a share41 against a close of $493.16, a discount of 1.6%. The cost of capital is 9.31%42, struck on market weights rather than book — the equity weight is 94.2% at today's capitalisation.

Three quarters of enterprise value sits in the terminal period, which is the honest weakness of the method here rather than a defect in the inputs. The two terminal methods disagree by 11.4%, and that spread is the width of the answer.

Recorded caveats
More than three quarters of the enterprise value is terminal value. The bridge from enterprise to equity value is net debt alone; associate holdings and pension deficits are not read off the balance sheet.
